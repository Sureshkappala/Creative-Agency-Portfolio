Billing & Engagement FAQs

Are these real client prices? ▼

No. These figures represent sample demo pricing for layout demonstration purposes. In real client engagements, budgets are tailored to bespoke deliverable scopes and technical requirements.

How are milestone payments structured? ▼

Standard fixed-price projects are divided into 50% deposit upon kickoff and 50% upon final deliverable sign-off. Monthly retainers are invoiced at the beginning of each billing cycle.

Can we upgrade or modify a package mid-project? ▼

Absolutely. We operate agile sprint backlogs. If your scope expands to include photography, 3D animations, or additional web pages, we seamlessly adjust the milestone schedule.
