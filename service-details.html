Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for this service? ▼

Most brand identity and design projects span 4 to 8 weeks depending on the scope of collateral and revision rounds. We operate in weekly iterative sprints with continuous stakeholder alignment.

Do we own the full intellectual property rights? ▼

Yes, 100%. Upon project completion and final milestone approval, all intellectual property, source files (Figma, AI, code repositories), and commercial rights transfer entirely to your organization.

How do revisions work? ▼

Our standard packages include 2 to 3 comprehensive revision rounds per milestone. Additional iteration rounds can be accommodated flexibly through our agile retainers.
