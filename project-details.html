Aura Spatial Audio

Client: Aura Sound Labs (Demo Client)

The Challenge

Project Overview & Objectives

Aura Sound Labs is a pioneering acoustic hardware startup developing spatial audio headphones. PixelCraft Studio was commissioned to build the brand identity from the ground up, including physical unboxing packaging, 3D renders, and an interactive e-commerce launch site.

Client Requirements

Create a minimalist yet emotionally resonant identity that conveys sonic precision and quiet luxury. The packaging required 100% recyclable molded fiber with custom metallic foil typography.

Tools & Technologies Employed

Figma Blender 3D Cinema 4D Illustrator Next.js
Strategic Solution

Creative Strategy & Design Process

We rooted the visual language in acoustic wave theory. Using electric purple gradients paired with deep velvety obsidian, the system translates sound frequencies into fluid visual art.

Design Process Breakdown

  1. 1. Sonic Acoustic Research: Waveform harmonics translation into modular vector curves.
  2. 2. Tactile Packaging Prototyping: Dieline engineering with FSC-certified pulp fiber.
  3. 3. 3D Spatial Renders: Photorealistic lighting passes in Cinema 4D and Redshift.
  4. 4. Web Platform Deployment: Smooth GSAP canvas scroll animations with zero lag.
